Frequently asked questions

My data doesn't show up on my other devices.

Menu & Groceries syncs your data with iCloud. Check that all your devices are signed in to the same iCloud account and that iCloud Drive is on in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Syncing can take a few minutes, especially the first time.

I changed devices: how do I get Premium back?

Premium is a one-time purchase, tied to your Apple Account and shared with your Family Sharing group. Open the Premium screen in the app and tap “Restore purchases”.

What does the free version include?

The shopping list, up to 15 recipes and the planning of the current week. Premium unlocks unlimited recipes, importing from a link or a photo, planning the weeks ahead, meal suggestions, the pantry, and recipe sharing and PDF export.

How do I import a recipe from a website?

In Safari, tap the Share button on the recipe page, then choose Menu & Groceries. You can also paste the link from the “+” button of the Recipes tab. Recipes exported from Paprika and Mela can be imported too.

How do I share the shopping list with my family?

In the Shopping tab, open the “…” menu and choose “Share with family”. The people you invite see and edit it from their own device, through iCloud.

How do I back up my recipes?

On top of iCloud sync, the app can export a full backup as a JSON file from “Backup” in the “…” menu of the Recipes tab. You can import it again at any time.

Privacy policy

Menu & Groceries · Last updated: October 25, 2026

In short: Menu & Groceries collects no personal data. Your recipes, planning, shopping list and pantry stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account. The developer has no access to them.

Data you enter

The recipes, photos, plans, shopping lists, stores, articles and pantry items you create are saved on your device. They are synced between your devices by iCloud, in the private database of your Apple Account. This data is handled by Apple under its privacy policy. The developer runs no server and cannot read it.

Shared shopping list

If you choose to share your shopping list, its content is shared through iCloud with the people you invite only. You can stop sharing at any time from the app.

Recipe import

When you import a recipe from a link, the app downloads that website's page directly to read the recipe. The website can see this request, as with any web visit. The link is not sent to any other service. The page address is kept with the recipe so that you can go back to it.

Importing from a photo uses Apple's text recognition, on your device. The photo is not sent anywhere.

Purchases

The Premium purchase is processed by the App Store. The developer receives neither your contact details nor your payment information.

Notifications

Reminders (cooking timers, items about to expire) are local notifications, scheduled on your device.

Tracking and advertising

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no trackers, and shares no data with third parties.

Deleting your data

Deleting a recipe or an item in the app also deletes it from iCloud. To erase everything, delete the app, then its iCloud data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.
